## Full Description
Spark uses AI to surface people you'll actually click with — based on shared interests, conversation style, and real compatibility signals, not just looks.

**Features:**
• **AI-Powered Matching** — learns from conversations, not just swipes
• **Real-Time Chat** — instant messaging with read receipts and typing indicators
• **Photo Verification** — verified profiles, fake accounts removed in minutes
• **Date Planner** — AI suggests personalized date ideas based on shared interests
• **Privacy First** — screenshot protection, full GDPR compliance with one-tap data export
• **Boost Your Profile** — get 10x more visibility when you need it most
• **Super Spark** — let someone know you're really interested

**How it works:**
1. Create your profile with photos and interests
2. AI curates matches based on compatibility, not just looks
3. Swipe right on people you're interested in
4. When you both swipe right, it's a match! Start chatting.
5. Use the Date Planner for personalized date ideas
